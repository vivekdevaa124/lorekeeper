# Session 1: The Gathering Storm

## The Prancing Pony
The party met at the Prancing Pony tavern in the town of Oakhaven. 
The tavern is run by a jolly, balding half-orc named **Garret "Keg-Smasher" Grog**. Despite his intimidating nickname, Garret is known for his sweet honey-mead and terrible jokes.

## The Quest Giver
A mysterious figure in a dark purple cloak approached the party. She introduced herself as **Elara Moonwhisper**, an elf scholar from the Silver Tower. 
She hired the party for 500 gold pieces to retrieve a stolen artifact: **The Sunstone Amulet**.

## The Sunstone Amulet
The amulet is said to glow with the light of a miniature sun. It was stolen two days ago by a gang of goblin thieves known as the **Red Tooth Clan**. 
Elara mentioned that the amulet is dangerous if exposed to direct moonlight for too long, as it might shatter and release a burst of chaotic energy.

## The Goblin Camp
The Red Tooth Clan is rumored to be hiding in the **Whispering Woods**, located a half-day's journey north of Oakhaven. The woods are known for strange, ghostly whispers that disorient travelers.
